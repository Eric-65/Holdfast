## 5. Showcase listing

**Name:** Holdfast Savings
**Category:** Save
**One-line pitch:** Savings goals that lock by staking NIM in your own wallet, with a friend keeping you honest.

**Description (about 100 words):**
Holdfast turns saving into staking. Create a goal (rent, school fees, a new laptop), and every NIM you save into it is staked from your own Nimiq Pay wallet: it earns rewards, never leaves your control, and takes a deliberate 12-to-24-hour withdrawal to spend. Share a link with one friend, your Goalkeeper, who sees your live progress, cheers you on with NIM and an on-chain message, and sees if you break the seal. Up to five goals share one stake, every lock is confirmed on the blockchain, and amounts show in NIM, USDT and naira. Profiles are wallet-signed, with invites and a referral leaderboard.

**Live:** https://holdfast-puce.vercel.app
**Repo:** https://github.com/Eric-65/Holdfast
**Demo video:** DEMO_VIDEO_URL

**How it uses NIM:**
- **Staking is the lock:** goals are created, held and released with Nimiq's staking transactions (create staker, add stake, deactivate, retire, remove).
- **On-chain confirmation:** every lock and withdrawal step is checked on the Nimiq blockchain before it counts.
- **Cheers with on-chain messages:** friends send NIM with a message that Holdfast reads back from the chain.
- **Wallet-signed profiles:** a Nimiq signed message proves ownership; no passwords.
- **Real usage, verified:** the public stats page counts only stake confirmed on chain.
